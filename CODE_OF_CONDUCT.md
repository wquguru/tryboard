# Code of Conduct

TryBoard is a learning project: many people here are writing their first firmware. We want it to be a place where
asking a basic question is normal and nobody is made to feel small for it.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in a second language.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons and data (a trace, a failing test, a datasheet page).
- Assume good faith, and accept correction gracefully when you are wrong.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first and give enough detail to reproduce a problem.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language, about anyone, for any reason.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when you
represent the project elsewhere.

Report problems privately to the maintainers at **hello@wqu.guru**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code,
in proportion to what happened.
